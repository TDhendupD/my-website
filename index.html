Who I am, in short.

My name is Tenzing. I study computer science and economics at Davidson College, and most of what I do outside class is building software people can actually use: a tool that forecasts when internship applications open, a browser extension that fills out long web forms for you, a finance app you can ask questions about your own spending.

In the summer of 2025 I worked at the ALPhA Lab, training point-cloud models on detector data from the Facility for Rare Isotope Beams. Since then I've built an AI study tool for a startup client and helped run Hack@Davidson.
